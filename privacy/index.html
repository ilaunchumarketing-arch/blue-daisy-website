Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Blue Daisy Lawn Care collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have — including how text messaging works.

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Blue Daisy Lawn Care ("Blue Daisy," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at bluedaisylawncare.com and provides lawn care and landscaping services in Central Florida. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle the personal information you share with us through our website, by phone, and by text message.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you request a free estimate, contact us, or sign up for service, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, service or property address, and any details you provide about your yard or the work you need.

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, we and our analytics and advertising partners may automatically collect your IP address, browser type, device information, the pages you view, and how you arrived at our site, using cookies and similar technologies (see Section 5).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your request, prepare an estimate, and schedule and provide our services.
  • To contact you by phone, email, or text message about your request, appointment, or account.
  • To send service reminders, follow-ups, and — only if you opt in — occasional offers or promotions.
  • To operate, improve, and secure our website, and to measure the performance of our advertising.
  • To comply with the law and protect our rights, customers, and business.

3. Text Messaging (SMS) & Calls

By providing your phone number and submitting a form or otherwise contacting us, you agree that Blue Daisy Lawn Care may call and send you text messages about your estimate, appointments, and service. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive marketing texts is not a condition of purchasing any service.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from us, and you can reply HELP for assistance. Mobile opt-in information and consent are never shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure and improve our advertising. These tools may collect information such as your device and browser, the pages you view, and actions you take on our site. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can manage ad preferences through the privacy controls offered by Meta and other platforms.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to run our business, including with:

  • Service providers who help us operate — for example, our customer-relationship and scheduling platform (GoHighLevel), website hosting, and communications providers — who are permitted to use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Advertising and analytics partners such as Meta, in connection with measuring and improving our ads.
  • Legal and safety recipients, when required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of others.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services, maintain our business records, and comply with legal obligations. When it is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • Texts: reply STOP to opt out at any time.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Access & deletion: you may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. We do not sell personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Third-Party Links

Our site may link to other websites we do not control. This policy applies only to Blue Daisy Lawn Care. Please review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of our website or services after a change means you accept the updated policy.
