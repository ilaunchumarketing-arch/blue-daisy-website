We Don't Just Cut Grass. We Care for Your Turf.

Blue Daisy Lawn Care is a locally owned lawn and landscape company built on a simple belief: every lawn in Central Florida deserves personalized, professional care — and every homeowner deserves to get their weekends back.

Our story

Rooted in Central Florida

Based in Haines City, we've spent 4+ years helping homeowners, HOA boards and small businesses across Clermont, Davenport, Four Corners and the surrounding communities keep their properties green and healthy.

We started Blue Daisy because we kept seeing the same thing: hardworking families paying "mow-and-go" crews who rushed through the yard, never treated the turf, and sometimes left it worse than they found it. We knew lawn care could be done right — and done in a way that's actually simple for the homeowner.

So we built our whole business around one all-inclusive monthly plan and a standard most companies skip: clean equipment before every job.

What we stand for

The Blue Daisy Difference

One Simple Plan

Mowing, edging, trimming, blowing and fertilization bundled into one monthly price. No surprise invoices, no add-on games.

Clean Equipment, Every Visit

We sanitize our mowers and trimmers before every job — because dirty equipment spreads weeds and turf disease between lawns. It's a standard, not a bonus.

Same Local Crew

You see the same familiar faces — not a different random crew every visit. We know your yard and we're accountable for it.

We Treat, Not Just Trim

We mow at the correct height for your grass type and feed your lawn so it gets healthier over time — instead of slowly thinning out.

Hablamos Español · Falamos Português

We proudly serve Central Florida's English-, Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking communities in the language you're most comfortable with.

Local & Accountable

We live and work here. Our reputation in your neighborhood is everything — and it shows in how we treat your property.
