Why homeowners switch to us

The Cheapest Mow in Town Can Cost You Your Whole Lawn

You've seen the "$99 a month, we'll mow anything" trucks. Here's what they don't tell you: many cut a dozen lawns a day and never wash their mower decks between yards.

  • Dirty equipment carries weed seeds and turf disease — crabgrass, dollarweed, brown patch and fungus — straight from a neighbor's sick lawn onto yours.
  • "Mow-and-go" crews don't fertilize, treat, or actually care for turf — so problems quietly get worse every visit.
  • Scalped, uneven cuts with dull blades stress the grass and invite even more weeds and pests.

Blue Daisy works differently. We keep equipment clean, mow at the correct height for your grass, and our monthly plan actually treats and feeds your lawn — so it gets better over time, not worse.
